Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "Period Tracker" (hosted at http://period.fishese.cc/ and its associated GitHub repository) handles your data.

1. Core Privacy Principle

Period Tracker is designed as a local-first, offline web application. We believe your data belongs to you. The developers of this application do not collect, transmit, store, or have access to any of your personal data, usage metrics, or tracked information.

2. Local Data Storage

All data you enter into the application is stored locally on your device within your web browser's storage (such as IndexedDB or LocalStorage).

3. Google Drive Backup Integration (drive.appdata)

To prevent data loss, the application offers an optional backup feature using your personal Google Drive account.

  • What we request: If you choose to enable backups, the app will request access to the drive.appdata scope via Google OAuth. This grants the app permission to read and write files strictly within a hidden, application-specific folder in your Google Drive. It does not grant the app access to your personal files, photos, or other Google Drive content.
  • How data is transferred: The backup process occurs directly between your local browser and Google's servers.
  • No Developer Access: At no point is your data routed through, stored on, or accessible by any servers owned or operated by the developers of this application.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Period Tracker's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking cookies, or diagnostic tools.

6. Changes to this Policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be reflected in the project's GitHub repository. Continued use of the application after changes constitute acceptance of the new policy.

7. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository at https://github.com/fishese/period-tracker.
